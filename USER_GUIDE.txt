1. GETTING STARTED (LOGIN & FIRST STEPS)
================================================================================

1.1  Open the app
     - Local development:  http://localhost:5173
     - Production:         http://<your-server-address>:5000

1.2  Log in
     - Enter the email and password given to you by the administrator.
     - Click "Sign In".

     Default accounts created by the setup script (CHANGE THESE AFTER FIRST LOGIN):
       Admin:        admin@hospital.com   / admin123
       (Your hospital may have created other accounts for you.)

1.3  First things to do after logging in
     - Click the lock icon (top-right, next to your name) to change your
       password to something only you know.
     - Look at the left sidebar: you will only see the modules your role is
       allowed to use. This is intentional.

1.4  Logging out
     - Click the power/logout icon at the bottom of the left sidebar.

1.5  Sidebar tips
     - Click the arrow button at the top of the sidebar to collapse/expand it.
     - The page title and today's date are shown in the top bar.

================================================================================
2. COMMON ACTIONS (USED BY EVERYONE)
================================================================================

2.1  Searching
     - Most list pages (Patients, Doctors, Invoices, etc.) have a search box
       at the top. Type a name, ID, or phone number and press Enter.

2.2  Forms
     - Fill in the fields and click the colored button at the bottom
       (usually "Save", "Create", or "Update").
     - Red text near a field means that field is required or invalid.

2.3  Status badges
     - Colored pills show the state of a record, for example:
         Green  = Active / Completed / Verified / Paid
         Amber  = Pending / In Progress / Ordered
         Red    = Cancelled / Rejected / Overdue / Low Stock
         Blue   = Scheduled / Admitted / Collected

2.4  Changing your password
     - Click the lock icon in the top-right corner.
     - Enter your current password, then your new password twice.
     - New password must be at least 6 characters.

3. ADMIN GUIDE
================================================================================

The Admin has access to every module. Your main responsibilities are
configuration, user management, and oversight.

3.1  Dashboard
     - Shows today's appointments, admissions, revenue, and quick stats.
     - Use the quick-action buttons to jump to common tasks.

3.2  Admin & Audit (IMPORTANT)
     - Manage user accounts: create users for doctors, receptionists,
       pharmacists, lab technicians, and patients.
     - Assign roles when creating a user. Roles cannot be changed casually.
     - View the audit log to see who did what and when. Review this
       regularly for security.

3.3  Staff
     - Add and manage hospital staff records (name, department, designation,
       contact, joining date).
     - Staff records are separate from login accounts.

3.4  Service Charges
     - Maintain the price catalog: consultation fees, lab tests, procedures,
       room charges, etc.
     - These charges are used when creating invoices, so keep them updated.

3.5  Inventory
     - Add stock items (medicines, consumables, equipment).
     - Create purchase orders when stock runs low.
     - Watch the low-stock alerts on the inventory page.

3.6  Reports
     - View revenue, patient, and operational reports.
     - Use filters (date range, department) to narrow results.

3.7  Billing & Insurance
     - Review invoices and payments.
     - Manage insurance policies and claims.

3.8  Housekeeping & Ambulance
     - Assign housekeeping tasks and track room cleaning status.
     - Manage the ambulance fleet and trip logs.

3.9  Best practices for admins
     - Change the default admin password immediately after setup.
     - Create one account per person - never share logins.
     - Deactivate accounts of staff who leave (do not delete, to keep audit history).
     - Review audit logs weekly.

4. DOCTOR GUIDE
================================================================================

Your daily workflow: see patients in OPD, review their history, write
prescriptions and lab orders, and manage admitted patients.

4.1  OPD & Queue
     - Open "OPD & Queue" to see today's waiting patients.
     - The queue shows appointments and walk-ins in order.
     - Click a patient to start/continue their consultation.

4.2  Patients
     - Search for a patient by name or UHID (e.g., HOSP-2026-000001).
     - Open a patient to see their full profile and history.

4.3  Medical Records / Encounters
     - During a consultation, create an Encounter (visit note):
       symptoms, diagnosis, vitals, treatment plan.
     - The patient's full medical history is available under Medical Records.

4.4  Prescriptions
     - Write prescriptions with medicine name, dosage, frequency, and duration.
     - The pharmacy will see the prescription and dispense the medicines.
     - Prescription status flow:
         Prescribed -> Dispensed (by pharmacist) -> Completed

4.5  Lab Orders
     - Order lab tests (blood, urine, etc.) for a patient.
     - The lab technician will collect the sample, run the test, and publish
       the result. You will see the result appear on the order.
     - Lab order status flow:
         Ordered -> Sample Collected -> Received -> Processing
         -> Verified -> Published

4.6  Radiology (RIS)
     - Order imaging studies (X-ray, CT, MRI, ultrasound).
     - View the radiologist's report when ready.

4.7  Blood Bank
     - Request blood units for a patient.
     - Check stock and compatibility before requesting.

4.8  Wards & IPD
     - Admit patients who need inpatient care; assign ward and bed.
     - Track the patient's stay and discharge when ready.

4.9  Operation Theatre
     - Schedule surgeries; record the procedure and outcome.

4.10 Nursing Station
     - Review nursing tasks and vitals recorded by the nursing team.

4.11 Diet & Nutrition
     - Create diet plans for admitted patients (normal, diabetic, low-salt, etc.).

4.12 Telemedicine
     - Conduct virtual consultations; schedule video calls with patients.

4.13 Emergency
     - See emergency cases, triage priority, and manage treatment.

4.14 Best practices for doctors
     - Always create an Encounter for every visit - it builds the patient's EMR.
     - Write clear prescriptions (dosage + frequency + duration).
     - Order lab tests from within the patient's record so results link back.

5. RECEPTIONIST GUIDE
================================================================================

You are the front desk. You handle registration, appointments, admissions,
billing, and front-office services.

5.1  Patient Registration
     - Open "Patients" -> "Add Patient".
     - Enter name, age/DOB, gender, phone, address, and emergency contact.
     - The system generates a unique UHID (e.g., HOSP-2026-000123).
     - Give the UHID to the patient - they will use it for all future visits.

5.2  Appointments (OPD)
     - Book appointments for patients with available doctors.
     - Walk-in patients can be added directly to the OPD queue.

5.3  OPD & Queue
     - Manage the daily queue: add walk-ins, mark patients as arrived,
       and call the next patient.

5.4  Billing
     - Create invoices for consultations, procedures, room charges, etc.
     - Record payments (cash, card, UPI) and print/give receipts.
     - Invoice status flow:
         Draft -> Issued -> Partially Paid -> Paid -> (Cancelled if needed)

5.5  Wards & IPD (Admissions)
     - Admit patients: select ward, bed, and admitting doctor.
     - Track bed availability on the ward page.

5.6  Emergency
     - Register emergency cases at the front desk with triage priority.

5.7  Ambulance
     - Log ambulance trips: destination, patient, driver, charges.

5.8  Insurance
     - Register patient insurance policies and file claims.

5.9  Housekeeping
     - Create housekeeping tasks for rooms and track completion.

5.10 Best practices for receptionists
     - Always search for an existing patient before registering a new one
       (avoid duplicate UHIDs).
     - Collect the UHID at every visit.
     - Issue invoices at the time of service to avoid billing backlogs.

================================================================================
6. PHARMACIST GUIDE
================================================================================

6.1  Pharmacy
     - Open "Pharmacy" to see prescriptions waiting to be dispensed.
     - For each prescription:
         a. Check the medicines and quantities.
         b. Verify stock availability (the system checks automatically).
         c. Dispense the medicines - status changes to "Dispensed".
     - If stock is insufficient, the system will warn you. Restock via
       Inventory (create a purchase order) or inform the admin.

6.2  Inventory
     - View stock levels and low-stock alerts.
     - Add new stock items.
     - Create purchase orders for replenishment.
     - Record stock received when the order arrives.

6.3  Best practices for pharmacists
     - Dispense only against a doctor's prescription.
     - Double-check dosage and patient name before handing over medicines.
     - Keep inventory updated in real time.

7. LAB TECHNICIAN GUIDE
================================================================================

7.1  Lab (LIS)
     - Open "Lab" to see all lab orders.
     - Orders arrive from doctors with status "Ordered".
     - Workflow:
         a. Ordered   - doctor has ordered the test
         b. Sample Collected - you collect the sample (blood/urine/etc.)
         c. Received  - sample reaches the lab
         d. Processing - test is running
         e. Verified  - you verify the result
         f. Published - result is visible to the doctor and patient
     - Each order has a barcode (e.g., LAB-20260214-000001) - use it to
       track the sample.

7.2  Blood Bank
     - Manage blood units: add donations, record blood group, expiry.
     - Fulfill blood requests from doctors.
     - Track stock by blood group.

7.3  Radiology (RIS)
     - Manage imaging orders and upload/record reports.

7.4  Best practices for lab technicians
     - Update the status at every step - doctors rely on it.
     - Verify results carefully before publishing.
     - Never publish a result without a sample.

8. PATIENT GUIDE
================================================================================

8.1  Your account
     - The hospital creates your login (email + password).
     - Change your password after first login (lock icon, top-right).

8.2  OPD & Queue
     - See your position in today's queue.

8.3  Medical Records
     - View your medical history, encounters, prescriptions, and lab results
       (once published by the lab).

8.4  Appointments
     - Book a follow-up appointment with your doctor.

8.5  Best practices for patients
     - Keep your UHID handy for every visit.
     - Update your phone number with the receptionist if it changes.

9. WORKFLOW DIAGRAMS
================================================================================

9.1  Lab Order Flow
     Doctor orders test
        |
        v
     Ordered -----------> Sample Collected -----------> Received
        |                                                 |
        |                                                 v
        |                                             Processing
        |                                                 |
        |                                                 v
        |                                             Verified
        |                                                 |
        |                                                 v
        +------------------------------------------> Published
                                                      (doctor & patient see result)

9.2  Prescription Flow
     Doctor prescribes
        |
        v
     Prescribed -----------> Dispensed (pharmacist) -----------> Completed

9.3  Invoice / Billing Flow
     Services rendered (consultation, lab, room, procedure)
        |
        v
     Draft -----------> Issued -----------> Partially Paid -----------> Paid
        |                                                              |
        +---------------------------- Cancelled -----------------------+

9.4  Patient Journey (full)
     Registration (UHID) -> OPD Appointment -> Consultation (Encounter)
        -> Prescription / Lab Order / Admission
        -> Pharmacy / Lab / Ward
        -> Billing & Payment -> Discharge / Follow-up

10. TROUBLESHOOTING & TIPS
================================================================================

10.1 "Cannot log in"
     - Check caps lock / email spelling.
     - Ask the admin to reset your password (admin can create a new password).

10.2 "I don't see a module I need"
     - The sidebar shows only modules for your role. If you genuinely need
       another module, ask the admin to change your role.

10.3 "Page shows an error"
     - Refresh the page. If it persists, note the error message and contact
       the administrator.

10.4 "Lab result not visible"
     - The result is only visible after the lab publishes it (status
       "Published"). Ask the lab technician if it is still processing.

10.5 General tips
     - Use the search boxes - they are faster than scrolling.
     - Keep your password private; never share accounts.
     - Log out when you leave your workstation.
